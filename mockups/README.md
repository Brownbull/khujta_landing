# khujta ai Design Mockups

This directory contains five distinct HTML/CSS mockups targeting different customer personas for khujta ai services.

## Mockup Files

| File | Target Persona | Design Emphasis |
|------|---------------|-----------------|
| `enterprise.html` | Enterprise Tech Company | Scalability, security, enterprise-grade solutions, case studies |
| `professional-services.html` | Legal/Professional Services | Strategic guidance disclaimer, compliance, expertise credentials |
| `healthcare.html` | Healthcare/Medical Organization | Data privacy, HIPAA awareness, patient-centered language |
| `startup.html` | Small Business/Startup | Agility, innovation, quick time-to-value, modern tech stack |
| `government.html` | Government/Defense Contractor | Security clearances, compliance, reliability, proven track record |

## Design Rationale

### 1. Enterprise Mockup (`enterprise.html`)
- **Color Palette**: Deep navy blue (#0f172a) conveys trust and professionalism
- **Typography**: Clean, modern sans-serif (Segoe UI) for readability
- **Visual Elements**: Subtle grid pattern, trust indicators (SOC 2, GDPR badges)
- **Content Focus**: Enterprise scalability, security compliance, case studies
- **CTA**: "Schedule a Consultation" - emphasizes business development

### 2. Professional Services Mockup (`professional-services.html`)
- **Color Palette**: Warm earth tones with gold accents (#b45309) convey tradition and expertise
- **Typography**: Georgia serif for headings adds authority and professionalism
- **Visual Elements**: Prominent disclaimer banner (amber background) for legal compliance
- **Content Focus**: Strategic guidance disclaimer, compliance awareness, credentials
- **CTA**: "Request Consultation" - emphasizes professional relationship

### 3. Healthcare Mockup (`healthcare.html`)
- **Color Palette**: Teal/seafoam (#134e4a) conveys health, calm, and trustworthiness
- **Typography**: Clean sans-serif (Inter) for modern healthcare feel
- **Visual Elements**: Privacy banner with lock icons, HIPAA awareness indicators
- **Content Focus**: Data privacy, HIPAA compliance, patient-centered approach
- **CTA**: "Learn More" - softer approach for healthcare decision makers

### 4. Startup Mockup (`startup.html`)
- **Color Palette**: Bold purple/pink gradient (#8b5cf6 to #ec4899) for innovation energy
- **Typography**: Modern Inter with bold headings for startup vibrancy
- **Visual Elements**: Floating gradient orbs, animated hover effects, tech stack badges
- **Content Focus**: Speed to value ($5-50 per feature), modern tech stack, live stats
- **CTA**: "Start Building" - action-oriented for fast-moving startups

### 5. Government Mockup (`government.html`)
- **Color Palette**: Deep navy with blue accents (#0c1222) conveys authority and security
- **Typography**: Conservative Segoe UI/Roboto for official appearance
- **Visual Elements**: Grid pattern, clearance badges, gold accents for importance
- **Content Focus**: Security clearances, compliance, documentation, proven track record
- **CTA**: "Contact Our Team" - emphasizes government procurement process

## Common Elements

All mockups include:
- **Hero Section**: Company name, tagline emphasizing AI agency expertise, CTA button
- **Services Overview**: Three distinct cards for each service category
- **Agent Portfolio**: Grid of 6 agent cards with domain tags
- **Contact Section**: khujta.ai@gmail.com email link
- **Responsive Design**: Mobile, tablet, and desktop breakpoints

## Design Principles

1. **Brand Coherence**: All mockups maintain khujta ai brand while adapting to audience expectations
2. **Inclusive Language**: Uses "guidance", "strategy", "insights" rather than "legal/tax advice"
3. **Strategic Disclaimer**: Prominent disclaimers on professional services mockup
4. **Accessibility**: Semantic HTML, sufficient color contrast, readable typography
